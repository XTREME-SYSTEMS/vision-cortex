## What the "Trio" is

1. **Vision Cortex** — the brain. A council of 13 AI agents that scans markets, validates opportunities, reverse-engineers competitors, paper-trades a $10M fund, and compounds a doctrine library. Currently on Base44; designed to be portable.
2. **AutoBuilder OS** (autobuilderos.com) — the hands. The build/launch engine that takes a validated Idea and ships a real product: scaffold, deploy, content, distribution. You own the domain; the new AutoBuilder is not connected yet — this playbook is the spec to connect it.
3. **Cloud Browser Engine** — the eyes. An isolated, proxy-routed headless browser that reads the live web for Shadow and the intel layer without fingerprinting your origin.

**The loop:** Vision Cortex finds and decides → Cloud Browser verifies → AutoBuilder OS builds and ships → revenue and outcomes feed back into Vision Cortex's doctrine brain → the loop sharpens. Forever.
